DM Rewind

Go back to any day in your Instagram chats.

Open your Instagram data export, pick a date, and land on that day's messages. No scrolling for an hour.

Or drag your export folder or .zip here. JSON and HTML exports both work.

Your messages stay on this computer. The page never uploads anything and works offline.

How do I get my Instagram export?
  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
  2. Choose Export your information (some versions say Download your information) and start a new export for your Instagram profile.
  3. Pick Export to device, then Customize information and keep only Messages to make it smaller and faster.
  4. Set the date range to All time. Format can be JSON or HTML; JSON gives exact timestamps.
  5. When Instagram emails you that it's ready, download the .zip. Open it here directly, or unzip it and open the folder (better for very large exports).

Exports don't include unsent or disappearing messages. A chat that's missing may be end-to-end encrypted, which Instagram keeps out of the standard export.
